How SWFC works

Most of the work happens before you arrive.

  1. 01

    Register

    Register through the conference registration site. Once registered, you receive hotel information and access to the live registration list.

  2. 02

    Reserve your room through SWFC

    SWFC manages hotel reservations for attendees, which gives you control and the best rate. Please do not call hotels directly.

  3. 03

    Book your appointments ahead

    With over 2,400 people attending, meeting on the floor is hard. Use the live registration list to contact people and request meetings before you arrive.

  4. 04

    Meet in your assigned location

    The association manages and assigns all company meeting locations. The conference app keeps the registration list, suites and meeting sites in your pocket.

Common questions

Before you go

When are 2027 registration fees set?+

Registration fees will be set in early March 2027. For reference, the pre-registration fee was $350 in 2026.

What is the refund policy?+

Full refunds are available before July 1st. No refunds after July 1st.

How do I book a hotel room?+

You must be registered to reserve a room. Hotel information comes with your registration confirmation. Please do not call hotels directly.

Can my spouse or guest come?+

Guests not participating in company meetings may attend at no charge, but please register them for planning. The Guest Program and Breakfasts require a ticket.

What should I wear?+

Business casual (no coats or ties) is recommended for the whole conference.
